## Tide pooling

Low tide on a winter morning is the best show in town. I keep a running list of what I find, and I'm slowly learning to tell the hermit crabs apart.

## Electronics and tinkering

Most of my projects start on the kitchen table with a breadboard and a bad idea. Some of them make it to the [projects page]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}).

## Cooking

I cook big batches on Sundays and try one new recipe a week. Current obsession: getting a sourdough starter to survive more than a month.

## Reading

Lately: science fiction, field guides, and books about how things are made.
