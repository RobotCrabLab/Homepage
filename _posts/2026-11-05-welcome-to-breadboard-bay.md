---
title: Welcome to Breadboard Bay
tags: [website]
---

Breadboard Bay is my corner of the internet, an imaginary bay where the sea life and the machines are built out of solderless breadboards. It's here to bring my two worlds together, electronics and the ocean, and to give my projects, résumé, writing and bird photos a home.

The site is built with Jekyll, which turns plain text files into ordinary web pages, and the artwork is drawn as SVG, so it stays sharp at any size. There's no server running code in the background. Each time I push a change to the site's repository, it rebuilds itself and the finished files are served as they are, with a little JavaScript so the pages can swap without the bay ever reloading.
