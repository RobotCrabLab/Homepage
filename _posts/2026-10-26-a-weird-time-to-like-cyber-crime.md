Several years ago, a friend recommended a podcast called *Darknet Diaries*. I listened for years, and from there I branched out to other cyber-focused podcasts like *Hacked* and *Risky Biz*. *Darknet Diaries* lands mostly in my "fact is greater than fiction" basket, and the stories are typically extraordinary. *Risky Biz* is a weekly podcast focused on current events and changing technology.

I've stumbled into this world at the exact time to watch AI break down the world's software stack as we know it. It has been fascinating to watch longstanding "secure" software get broken by obscure bugs and vulnerabilities. It took me years to understand hacker shorthand like on-prem, zero-days, and RCE (and sometimes it feels like I've only got those right 50% of the time), but it's been an absolute stroke of luck to stumble in at the right time.

If you're at all intrigued, here's an episode of *Darknet Diaries* that I found fascinating, plus links to the other podcasts to stay up to date with the rapidly changing, AI-fueled cybersecurity landscape.

## Start here

- **[Ep 36: Jeremy from Marketing](https://darknetdiaries.com/episode/36/)**, *Darknet Diaries*. The new guy at the office isn't quite who he seems.

## Stay up to date

- **[Darknet Diaries](https://darknetdiaries.com/)**: true stories from the dark side of the internet, hosted by Jack Rhysider.
- **[Hacked](https://www.hackedpodcast.com/)**: strange tales of hacking, tech, internet grifters, AI, and security, with Jordan Bloemen and Scott Francis Winder.
- **[Risky Business](https://risky.biz/)**: a weekly information security podcast covering the week's news, with interviews from people across the industry.
